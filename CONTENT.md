# Marothi — Content Strategy

## 1. Content philosophy
- **Top layer** (landing page, and the top of each service page) is skimmable: an interaction plus one or two lines of copy.
- **Deep layer** (expandable panels, and the dedicated service pages themselves) carries the full explanatory and SEO-supporting text.
- Every interactive proof point ends in a specific call to action tied to what was just shown — never a generic "contact us."

## 2. Landing page content plan
1. **Hero** — WhatsApp bot live time/value calculator, a brand-line headline (for example "small drops. lasting impact."), one-line subhead, primary CTA (start a WhatsApp bot demo / get a quote).
2. **Service teaser: WhatsApp Bots** — light version of the calculator or the simulated chat, one to two lines of benefit copy, "See how it works" link through.
3. **Service teaser: Professional Email / Custom Software** — spam-to-inbox toggle or the before/after slider, one to two lines, link through.
4. **Service teaser: Websites** — speed meter, one to two lines, link through.
5. **Service teaser: Software and IoT Supply** — live dashboard mockup, one to two lines, link through.
6. **Social proof strip** — two to three SME peer testimonials.
7. **Trust strip** — POPIA note, response-time promise, local-support note.
8. **Footer CTA** — WhatsApp click-to-chat plus contact details.

## 3. Service page template (applies to all four, content varies)
1. **Hero** — full interactive demo for that service, one-line value statement, primary CTA.
2. **Short video** (30-60s, optional, never autoplay) — include only if it adds something the interaction alone can't show.
3. **"Read more" expandable panels** — two to three panels, each answering one specific question (for example "how long does setup take," "what does it cost," "what do I need to provide") rather than one long block of text.
4. **Case study** — one real, or realistic placeholder, SME story, told briefly.
5. **Pricing indicator** — a starting-from price or a clearly explained pricing logic, not a black box.
6. **FAQ** (optional, SEO-supporting).
7. **Final CTA** — a specific action: book a call, start on WhatsApp, request a quote.

## 4. Page-specific content notes

### WhatsApp Bots (flagship)
- Lead stat: hours and rand saved per week.
- Industry-specific proof: restaurant, plumbing, or type-your-own demo.
- Case study angle: a local SME that stopped missing after-hours enquiries.

### Professional email
- Lead stat: inbox delivery rate and open rate.
- One line explaining why a branded-domain email is treated differently by spam filters than a free gmail address.

### Custom software and apps
- Lead visual: before/after slider (manual process vs. custom tool).
- Copy focuses on time and error reduction, not technical architecture.

### Websites
- Lead visual: load-speed comparison.
- Tie the copy back to the brand philosophy: a fast site is a form of respect for the visitor's data and time.

### Software and IoT supply
- Lead visual: live dashboard mockup.
- Translate "Telemetrics / Sage / Microsoft Suite / IoT" into plain outcomes ("know what's happening in your business in real time"), not a feature list.

## 5. Tone of voice
- Plain, direct, sentence case, no corporate filler ("leverage," "seamless," "unlock").
- Written for a busy SME owner skimming on a phone — short sentences, concrete numbers over adjectives.
- South African English conventions; avoid jargon that assumes an IT background.

## 6. SEO approach
- Homepage and teaser sections stay light — optimised for conversion, not keyword volume.
- Full keyword-rich, structured, indexable text lives on the four service pages and any case study or blog content.
- Each service page owns a clear primary keyword cluster (for example "whatsapp bot south africa," "professional business email south africa").

## 7. Social proof strategy
- Real SME owners prioritised over enterprise logos, matching the target audience.
- Format: short quote, name, business type, and city — the city adds local credibility.

## 8. Pricing communication
- Show a "from R.." indicator per service wherever possible.
- Reserve "request a quote" for genuinely bespoke work, such as custom software or large IoT integrations.
