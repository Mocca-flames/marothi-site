MAROTHI -- IMAGE, ICON AND SVG ASSET LIST
==========================================

1. PHOTOGRAPHY (primary visual layer)
--------------------------------------
Style: real, local, SME-representative. A real person holding a phone or
using the product in a real business setting. No generic or stereotyped
stock imagery.

Sourcing: commission a local South African photographer where budget
allows. Use curated stock (for example Nappy, or Unsplash's African/Black
imagery collections) only as a launch placeholder.

Needed shots (priority order):
[ ] Landing page hero -- SME owner holding a phone, with a chat-style
    interface visible on screen (generic message-bubble styling, not a
    literal WhatsApp trademark reproduction)
[ ] WhatsApp Bots service page -- restaurant owner and plumber, one shot
    each, matching the two demo industries
[ ] Professional Email service page -- business owner at a laptop, a
    branded email visible
[ ] Custom Software page -- business owner using a tablet or dashboard
[ ] Websites page -- business owner viewing their new site on phone and
    desktop
[ ] Software and IoT Supply page -- business owner near a device or
    dashboard screen
[ ] Testimonial headshots (three to five, once real testimonials are
    collected)

Format and technical notes:
- Deliver as WebP with a JPEG fallback, minimum 2x resolution for the
  largest display size
- Served through next/image, lazy-loaded below the fold
- Aspect ratios: 4:5 for card imagery; hero ratio to be confirmed against
  final layout

2. ART ACCENTS (procedural, blue, painterly)
---------------------------------------------
Style: abstract layered blue washes -- no gradients, flat overlapping
shapes at varying opacity -- evoking watercolour or ink rather than
corporate flat-vector illustration.

Usage rule: one accent per image or section, single corner placement,
rotated and organic, never centred, never behind body text.

Needed variants:
[ ] Hero accent (large, roughly 140-200px footprint)
[ ] Service card accent (smaller, roughly 100-120px footprint)
[ ] Three to four alternate compositions so repeated sections don't look
    identical
[ ] Droplet/ripple SVG for the signature click micro-interaction
    (already prototyped -- an expanding circle, stroke only, no fill)

Format and technical notes:
- Deliver as inline SVG, not raster, so colour follows the design token
  system
- Keep file size minimal -- these are accents, not full illustrations

3. ICONS
---------
Icon set: Tabler Icons, outline style only (no filled variants).

Already used in prototypes:
- ti-photo            image placeholder
- ti-check / ti-circle-check   confirmation, verified sender
- ti-alert-triangle    spam or warning state
- ti-mail              email
- ti-message-circle    chat / automation (generic, not the WhatsApp logo)
- ti-clock             time-saved messaging
- ti-device-mobile     phone / app references

Likely additional needs (confirm during build):
[ ] ti-map-pin          location / service area
[ ] ti-calendar         bookings
[ ] ti-currency-dollar or a custom Rand mark   pricing
[ ] ti-shield-check     POPIA / trust badge

4. LOGO AND FAVICON
---------------------
[ ] Primary logo (SVG, scalable, works on light backgrounds)
[ ] Reversed or light logo for dark or accent-coloured sections
[ ] Favicon set (16x16, 32x32, 180x180 for iOS, 512x512 for PWA/manifest)
[ ] Decide whether the logo itself should reference the water droplet
    motif directly, so it reads as one language with the art accents

5. WHAT TO AVOID
-------------------
- Flat corporate vector illustration (the style explicitly being
  replaced)
- Stock photography that reads as generic or international rather than
  local South African
- The literal WhatsApp logo or an exact reproduction of WhatsApp's UI
  colour and style -- the chat demo uses a generic message-bubble style
  instead
- Autoplay video, anywhere on the site
- Decorative gradients -- flat colour and opacity layering only, per the
  design system
