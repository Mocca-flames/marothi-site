MAROTHI -- IMAGE, ICON AND SVG ASSET LIST
==========================================

1. PHOTOGRAPHY (primary visual layer)
--------------------------------------
Style: real, local, SME-representative. A real person holding a phone or
using the product in a real business setting. No generic or stereotyped
stock imagery.

Sourcing: commission a local South African photographer where budget
allows. Use curated stock (for example Nappy, or Unsplash's African/Black
imagery collections) only as a launch placeholder.

Roles per service page (guided 11-step template, see CONTENT.md Section 3):
- Scene-setting "pain" shot (step 02) -- the owner in the exact pain
  moment; the emotional anchor of the page. 4:5.
- Explainer detail (step 03) -- close crop (hands on phone, screen detail),
  often a crop of the scene shot. 4:5.
- Scenario cards x3 (step 04) -- one per industry, must match the demo
  presets. 4:5.
- Testimonial headshots (step 08) -- shared across pages. 4:5.
- CTA "relief" background (step 11) -- same person and location as step 02,
  now relaxed (the "after" shot); can be a second take in the same session.
  16:9 full-bleed.
- Alt text must describe the scene AND its narrative role (e.g. "restaurant
  owner checking missed messages on a phone at the counter, evening light").

  against final layout

2. ART ACCENTS (procedural, blue, painterly)
---------------------------------------------
Style: abstract layered blue washes -- no gradients, flat overlapping
shapes at varying opacity -- evoking watercolour or ink rather than
corporate flat-vector illustration.

Usage rule: one accent per image or section, single corner placement,
rotated and organic, never centred, never behind body text.

Needed variants:
[ ] Hero accent (large, roughly 140-200px footprint)
[ ] Service card accent (smaller, roughly 100-120px footprint)
[ ] Three to four alternate compositions so repeated sections don't look
    identical
[ ] Droplet/ripple SVG for the signature click micro-interaction
    (already prototyped -- an expanding circle, stroke only, no fill)

Format and technical notes:
- Deliver as inline SVG, not raster, so colour follows the design token
  system
- Keep file size minimal -- these are accents, not full illustrations

5. WHAT TO AVOID
-------------------
- Flat corporate vector illustration (the style explicitly being
  replaced)
- Stock photography that reads as generic or international rather than
  local South African
- The literal WhatsApp logo or an exact reproduction of WhatsApp's UI
  colour and style -- the chat demo uses a generic message-bubble style
  instead
- Autoplay video, anywhere on the site
- Decorative gradients -- flat colour and opacity layering only, per the
  design system
